Billing & Subscription Policy

Effective September 6, 2026 · Version 1.1

Subscriptions and checkout are currently under maintenance. Oryntra is not currently offering a public paid checkout through this product.

Current access labels

References to Base, Oryntra Pro, or Max Bundle are product-access labels or planned feature comparisons. They are not, by themselves, an offer for sale, proof of payment, or an entitlement to a future feature. Owner-only diagnostic entitlements are test states and do not create a purchase, contract, customer subscription, or refund right.

Future purchases

Before any paid subscription becomes available, Oryntra will present the applicable price, billing cadence, renewal terms, included limits, cancellation instructions, and refund information at the point of purchase. A future launch may have terms that differ by platform, region, or provider.

Apple purchases

If a subscription is offered through the iOS app, billing, cancellation, and eligible refund requests will be handled through Apple’s purchase flow and the applicable Apple policies. Oryntra cannot independently refund an Apple in-app purchase outside that process.

Questions

For a billing question or to report an incorrect access state, use the contact page. Do not send passwords, payment-card information, or market-data-provider keys.
