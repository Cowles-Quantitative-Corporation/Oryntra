Oryntra explains software mechanics and research evidence; it does not claim validated alpha, future performance, or personal suitability.

Public research

Public scanner outputs show deterministic evidence, setup context, patterns, and hypothetical rule-derived scenario levels. Public Quant Lab demonstrations use frozen published profiles and fixed constraints. They do not use a user’s account value, holdings, income, objectives, tax status, or risk tolerance.

Internal research

CQC internal research, when enabled, is separately authorized server-side and is not a subscriber feature. Its research artifacts, configuration controls, and ledgers are not public product outputs.

Limitations

Inputs can be missing, delayed, stale, adjusted, or incorrect. Data rights are separate from product ownership. Historical testing depends on a declared universe, dates, assumptions, costs, and implementation choices; it cannot establish future profitability.
