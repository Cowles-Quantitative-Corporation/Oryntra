1. Current plans

Oryntra Pro is currently $6.99 per month on the web. CQC Max is currently $10.99 per month on the web. A payment method is required for the 7-day free trial. Unless canceled before the trial ends, the selected plan begins billing at the displayed monthly price and renews monthly until canceled. Taxes may apply where required.

Free access may have lower usage limits and may include advertising. Paid access may include higher limits, premium features such as Portfolio Lab where specified, and an ad-free experience where the applicable plan states that benefit.

2. Cancellation

Canceling during the trial prevents the first recurring charge. Canceling after billing begins prevents future renewal. Unless otherwise stated at cancellation, access continues through the end of the current paid billing period.

3. Refunds

Completed subscription charges are not automatically refundable merely because a subscription is canceled after billing. Refunds, credits, or corrections may be provided where required by law or where Oryntra determines that a billing error occurred. If a purchase is made through an app store or another platform, that platform’s refund and cancellation rules control.

4. Plan and price changes

Oryntra may change plan features, quotas, or prices prospectively. Material changes to a paid subscription should be communicated before the affected renewal where reasonably possible. The checkout page and customer portal show the terms applicable to a specific subscription.

5. No investment interest

A subscription purchases software access only. It does not provide equity, profit sharing, revenue sharing, investment participation, ownership in CQC research, or a right to any proprietary trading result.

6. Test entitlements

Developer, owner, beta, diagnostic, or manually assigned test entitlements are not purchases and do not create a customer payment, contract, subscription, refund right, or investment interest.
