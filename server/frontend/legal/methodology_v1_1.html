Methodology & Limits

Effective September 6, 2026 · Version 1.1

This page explains product mechanics, not a validated investment methodology or a performance claim. Oryntra does not promise that any model, signal, or portfolio directive will be profitable or suitable for you.

Data and inputs

Features may analyze market bars and other data supplied by an enabled provider, a user-selected dataset, or locally entered information. Coverage, timing, adjustments, corporate actions, and availability vary by source. Missing, delayed, stale, or incorrect inputs can change outputs. Provider terms and data licenses apply separately.

Research outputs

The product can calculate rule-based technical measures, scenario comparisons, hypothetical risk plans, research backtests, paper records, and model scores. Portfolio Lab records a hypothetical next-session action for research; it does not connect to a brokerage, transmit an order, monitor a brokerage account, or produce a live execution result.

Evaluation limits

Historical testing and holdouts are useful diagnostics, not proof of future alpha or reliability. Results depend on the selected universe, dates, assumptions, rebalance schedule, fees, slippage, liquidity, benchmark, and implementation choices. Real-world conditions can differ materially.

Use carefully

Read the risk disclosure before using any output. Independently verify information and do not treat research labels, access tiers, or visuals as financial advice.
