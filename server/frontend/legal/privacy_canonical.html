1. Information Oryntra handles

  • Account data: email address, display name, salted password hash, legal-consent version, login/session information, account status, and subscription status.
  • Research records: watchlists, paper-journal entries, notes, saved research settings, Portfolio Lab directives or lifecycle records, model selections, usage/quota records, and saved derived results where a feature provides saving.
  • Market-analysis requests: requested symbols, selected settings, and normalized market bars submitted for a calculation. Browser-direct research paths are designed to process raw bars transiently and not persist raw uploaded OHLCV unless a feature clearly states otherwise.
  • Billing: Stripe customer/subscription references, subscription status, invoice/payment state, and related billing metadata needed to enforce access. Oryntra does not store full payment-card numbers.
  • Service and security data: request metadata, network/device/browser information, authentication events, abuse-prevention information, and error logs.
  • Support communications: information you choose to send us.

2. Provider keys and third-party market data

On the web, a connected Polygon / Massive or Twelve Data key is stored in browser storage on your device and sent directly to the selected provider. Oryntra does not receive, store, log, or return the key. The provider receives your key and request under its own terms and privacy policy.

TradingView receives the symbol and related chart configuration needed to render its hosted chart under its own privacy practices. Market-data providers and chart services may collect information independently from Oryntra.

3. Demo data

The public demo may use fixed simulated or illustrative display data. Demo use may still generate ordinary service, security, or analytics logs. If a live TradingView chart is shown beside demo results, the chart interaction is handled separately by TradingView and is not the source of the simulated Oryntra demo output.

4. How information is used

Oryntra uses information to authenticate users, operate requested features, retain user-created research records, enforce limits and subscriptions, prevent abuse, troubleshoot, communicate service information, process billing, comply with legal obligations, and improve service reliability.

5. Service providers and disclosures

Information may be processed by hosting, database, email, security, payment, market-data, chart, analytics, and advertising providers only as needed for their functions. Oryntra may also disclose information when required by law, to protect users or the service, to investigate abuse, or as part of a business transfer.

Free users may see ads or sponsorships. Advertising providers may process limited browser/device information under their own policies. Oryntra does not intentionally provide advertising partners with passwords, provider API keys, full payment-card numbers, or private research notes merely to display an ad.

6. Retention and deletion

Account and user-created data are retained while an account is active and afterward only as reasonably needed for backups, security, dispute handling, fraud prevention, accounting, or legal obligations. Subscription and transaction records may be retained as required for billing, tax, accounting, or legal purposes.

Account deletion removes Oryntra-controlled account data through the available deletion process subject to those limits. It does not delete records independently held by Stripe, TradingView, a market-data provider, an advertising provider, or another third party.

7. Security

Oryntra uses password hashing, authenticated sessions, access controls, restricted public serialization, and encrypted transport in production. Provider keys in the browser-direct flow remain on your device and are sent directly to the selected provider. No system is completely secure. Do not email passwords, API keys, payment-card data, authentication codes, database files, or session tokens.

8. Your choices

  • Update or delete supported watchlists, paper-journal entries, notes, and saved research records.
  • Clear a provider key from Settings or browser storage.
  • Manage or cancel direct web subscriptions through the Stripe customer portal when available.
  • Request access, correction, or deletion through {{LEGAL_CONTACT_EMAIL}}, subject to identity verification and legal limitations.

9. Children

Oryntra is not directed to children under 13. A person unable to enter a binding agreement may use Oryntra only with any legally required parent or guardian authorization.

10. Changes

We may update this policy when the service, subscriptions, advertising, providers, or applicable requirements change. The current version and effective date will appear on this page.
