Information handled

Oryntra handles account identity and session data; watchlists; paper-journal records; research settings; saved simulation metadata; user-selected tickers; submitted market bars when applicable; and service security, device/browser, and error information. Browser-uploaded raw bars are processed for the requested research path and are not retained where the feature identifies them as transient.

Keys and service providers

Provider keys remain on the device—browser storage on the web and secure device storage on iOS—and go directly to the selected provider. Oryntra does not receive those keys. Where enabled, TradingView, market-data providers, hosting, Apple, payment providers, Google Mobile Ads, and consent tooling process information under their own terms and policies.

Use, retention, and deletion

Information is used to provide requested features, authenticate accounts, retain user-created records, apply limits, secure and troubleshoot the service, and meet applicable obligations. Account deletion removes Oryntra-controlled account data through the available deletion control, subject to reasonable backup, security, dispute, and legal retention. It does not delete data held independently by third parties.

Children and contact

Oryntra is not directed to children under 13. A person unable to enter a binding agreement may use it only with required parental or guardian authorization. For privacy questions or requests, contact {{LEGAL_CONTACT_EMAIL}}. Do not send passwords or provider keys.
