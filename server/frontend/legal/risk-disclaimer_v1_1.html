Oryntra is an educational, research, and planning product—not a broker, investment adviser, fiduciary, exchange, custodian, or trading venue. It cannot place, route, cancel, or manage orders or money.

What the product may show

Scans, scores, charts, risk plans, pattern labels, watchlists, paper-trading records, Quant Lab reports, and Portfolio Lab directives are model-generated research outputs. Words such as “buy,” “sell,” “hold,” “trim,” “entry,” “stop,” “target,” or “exit” describe a hypothetical rule or plan only. They are not personalized investment advice, a recommendation, a solicitation, or a promise of a result.

Historical and simulated results

Backtests, paper portfolios, validation summaries, model comparisons, alpha, win-rate, benchmark, and risk statistics use historical, supplied, delayed, incomplete, or simulated inputs. They may contain assumptions, survivorship bias, data errors, model-selection bias, look-ahead errors, execution assumptions, and costs that differ from live conditions. They do not predict future performance and should not be used as a basis for trading.

Execution and market risk

Prices can move sharply or gap through a planned stop or target. Stops, trailing stops, risk limits, correlation overlays, volatility adjustments, liquidity assumptions, and market-regime signals do not guarantee a fill, limit a loss, diversify risk, or protect capital. Illiquid securities, outages, delayed data, corporate actions, and market stress can make any displayed plan inaccurate or unusable. You can lose some or all of any money invested.
