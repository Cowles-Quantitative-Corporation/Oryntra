Privacy Policy

Effective September 6, 2026 · Version 1.1
Summary: Oryntra needs account and user-created research records to operate. It does not require a brokerage connection. Provider API keys stay on your device and go directly to the provider, not to Oryntra.

1. Information Oryntra handles

  • Account data: email address, display name, salted password hash, legal-consent version, login/session information, and subscription status where applicable.
  • Research records: watchlists, paper-trade entries, notes, saved Portfolio Lab decision directives, configuration and dataset fingerprints, and account quota records.
  • Service and security data: request metadata, error information, and network/device information processed by Oryntra or its hosting/security providers to operate, secure, and troubleshoot the service.
  • Market-analysis requests: the ticker and normalized market bars needed for a requested browser- or app-direct calculation. The browser-direct and Portfolio Lab paths are designed not to persist raw uploaded OHLCV bars; saved records retain derived directives and reproducibility metadata instead.
  • Support communications: information you choose to send us.

2. Provider keys and third-party market data

On the web, a connected provider key is stored in that browser’s local storage. On iOS, it is stored through the device secure-storage facility. In both cases it is sent directly to Polygon / Massive or Twelve Data; Oryntra does not receive, store, log, or use the key. The provider receives your key and request under its own terms and privacy policy. TradingView receives the symbol needed to render its hosted chart under its own privacy policy.

3. Advertising and consent

The iOS app includes Google Mobile Ads and Google’s consent tooling. When ads are enabled and the applicable consent flow permits them, Google and its partners may process data under their own policies. Use the iOS app’s Privacy choices control to revisit available advertising choices. This policy does not replace the App Store privacy disclosure, which must accurately identify data collected by Oryntra and every included third-party SDK for the released app version.

4. How information is used and shared

Oryntra uses information to authenticate users, provide requested features, retain user-created records, enforce limits and licenses, prevent abuse, troubleshoot, comply with law, and communicate about the service. Information may be processed by hosting, database, identity, security, email, advertising, payment, AI, market-data, and chart providers when needed for their services. Oryntra does not sell passwords or provider keys.

5. Retention and deletion

Account and user-created data are retained while an account is active and afterward only as reasonably needed for backups, security, disputes, or legal obligations. Use the in-app or website account-deletion control to delete the account from Oryntra-controlled systems, subject to those limits. Deletion does not remove data independently held by Apple, Google, TradingView, a market-data provider, or another service you used. Clearing a provider key from Settings—or clearing the app/browser storage—removes it from that device.

6. Your choices and contact

You can update your research records, clear a provider key, use the in-app Privacy choices control where available, and request access, correction, or deletion by contacting silasproff@gmail.com. We may need to verify a request and may retain limited information where law permits or requires. Do not send credentials or payment data by email.

7. Children and changes

Oryntra is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their personal information without any required parental authorization. We may update this policy when the service or applicable requirements change; the current version and effective date will appear here.
